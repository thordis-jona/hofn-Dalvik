A classic Icelandic swimming-pool experience, with a 25-metre pool, hot tubs, a children's pool, waterslide and steam room. Mountain views make a swim here especially enjoyable. It is equally suited to unwinding after an outdoor adventure or spending a relaxed day with the family.
