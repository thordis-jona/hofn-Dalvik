The house at Karlsrauðatorg 4 offers 135 square metres across three floors. It has been carefully renovated in keeping with its age by craftspeople who specialise in conserving older houses. The result is an exceptionally beautiful and welcoming home with four bedrooms, two bathrooms, a sitting room and dining room with a wood-burning stove, a well-equipped kitchen, and laundry and storage space in the basement.

The structure, roof, cladding, windows, wiring, radiators, plumbing, floors and drainage have all been renewed, among other improvements. At the same time, the house's original character has been preserved through features such as exposed ceiling beams, the sitting-room floor and the original staircase between floors.
